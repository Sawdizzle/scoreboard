Scoreboard v5.0 is Scoreboard rebuilt around the team. Score the game once, on your phone: the scorebug on the stream, the team's season stats and a public page for every game come from the same taps.

**New**
- Teams with five staff seats (organizer, head coach, coach, scorer, media), filled by one-use invite links.
- Rosters and seasons. Every play names its batter and pitcher, so season lines (AVG, OBP, SLG, OPS, ERA, WHIP, W/L/SV) build themselves from scored games, on top of a GameChanger season import.
- Public team pages: schedule and results, season stats, the roster, and a live page for each game, with each player shown the way the staff chose.
- Stat cards that slide out from behind any scorebug: batter, pitcher, due up, leaders, box score, lineup, defense, matchup, sponsor, and the mid-inning board.
- The Stream screen: looks, layout, theme, sound, scenes, sponsors, QR code and position in one place, over a live preview.
- Opponents remembered with their last lineup; a jersey-number keypad for the lineup card; password reset; an optional invite code for sign-up.

**Carried over from v4**
- Fourteen looks, now including MLBFox; v4's seventeen themes and five layouts with custom colors for Scoreboard's own bug; nine positions.
- Every sport in full: football downs and distance, soccer cards and stoppage time, basketball fouls and bonus, volleyball serve and sets, and the game clocks.
- Sound in five synthesized packs; the walk-off and the other stingers; moments and rally from the pad.
- Starting Soon with venue weather, Final, Game paused with a storm and thunder, the ticker, Elevated scenes, the looks' intros.
- OBS control from the pad: replays and auto-clip, cameras, go live, record, the buffer.
- The pad: keyboard keys, the screen kept awake, every tap saved in order through a weak signal, two phones in step, Demo.

**Moving from v4**
- `/control`, `/overlay?ch=<token>` and `/recap?game=<id>` keep working; the Browser Source in OBS needs no change.
- v4's games came over with their scores, line scores, lineups and plays; its saved teams are remembered opponents.
- v4 stays at tag `v4.47`.
